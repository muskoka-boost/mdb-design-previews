The practical details.

Answers from the published seasonal menu to help you prepare your enquiry.

When is the seasonal holiday menu in effect?

The published seasonal holiday menu is in effect from November 15 to January 15. The page identifies its holiday banquet selection as the 2026 menu. Confirm your event date and availability directly with La Distinction.

What is the minimum group size?

The seasonal holiday menu specifies a minimum of 10 people. Include your approximate guest count when you enquire so an advisor can discuss your gathering.

What should I share about dietary requirements?

Tell La Distinction about guests’ allergies, intolerances and special diets when requesting a quote. The seasonal menu page describes adjustments for these requirements and an allergy-management fee. Discuss the specific needs and applicable charges with an advisor.

Which format suits a seated corporate dinner?

The banquet is a plated, three-to-five-course meal served at the tables and paced for a seated event. The hot buffet is self-serve, more relaxed and flexible. La Distinction recommends choosing according to your event’s style and number of guests.

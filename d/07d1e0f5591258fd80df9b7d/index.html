A few practical details to help you prepare.

When is the seasonal holiday menu in effect?

The published seasonal holiday menu is in effect from November 15 to January 15. The page identifies its holiday banquet selection as the 2026 menu. Confirm the menu and availability for your intended date with an advisor.

What is the minimum group size?

The seasonal holiday menu specifies a minimum of 10 people. Include your approximate guest count when you enquire so an advisor can discuss your gathering.

Which format suits a seated corporate dinner?

The banquet is a plated, three-to-five-course meal served at the tables. The hot buffet is self-serve and described as more relaxed and flexible. Choose according to your event’s style and number of guests, with advice from La Distinction.

What should I share about dietary requirements?

Tell La Distinction about guests’ allergies, intolerances and special diets when requesting a quote. The seasonal menu page describes adjustments and an allergy-management fee. Discuss the specific needs and applicable charges with an advisor rather than assuming a dish will be suitable.

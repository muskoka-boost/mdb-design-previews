Before you
set the table.

A few practical details to help you prepare your enquiry. The official menu notes that menus and prices may change without notice.

When is the seasonal menu in effect?

The published seasonal holiday menu is in effect from November 15 to January 15. The page identifies its holiday banquet selection as the 2026 menu. Confirm your intended event date with an advisor.

What is the minimum group size?

The seasonal holiday menu specifies a minimum of 10 people. Include your approximate guest count when you enquire so an advisor can discuss your gathering.

Which format suits a seated corporate dinner?

The banquet is a plated, three-to-five-course meal served at the tables. The hot buffet is self-serve and described as more relaxed and flexible. La Distinction recommends choosing according to your event’s style and number of guests.

What about allergies and special diets?

Tell La Distinction about guests’ allergies, intolerances and special diets when requesting a quote. The seasonal menu page describes adjustments and an allergy-management fee. Discuss specific requirements and applicable charges with an advisor rather than assuming any dish will suit every guest.

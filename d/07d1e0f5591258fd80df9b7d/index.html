La Distinction offers catering for weddings, receptions and corporate events, from Joliette to the Montréal region. Its holiday selection carries that gastronomic focus into the season, drawing on products from Quebec and Lanaudière.

The seasonal menu changes each holiday season. The banquet and hot-buffet formats are offered year-round; it is the dishes that change. For a workplace or organizational gathering, the first decision is the style of meal you want your guests to share.

Before you enquire

Good questions
for a gathering.

When is the seasonal menu in effect?

The published seasonal holiday menu is in effect from November 15 to January 15. The official page identifies the holiday banquet selection as the 2026 menu. Ask an advisor about your preferred event date and availability.

How many people can we plan for?

The seasonal holiday menu specifies a minimum of 10 people. Share your approximate guest count so an advisor can discuss your gathering and the meal format that fits it.

Which format suits a seated corporate dinner?

The banquet is a plated, three-to-five-course meal served at the tables and paced for a seated event. The hot buffet is self-serve, more relaxed and flexible. La Distinction recommends choosing according to the event’s style and number of guests.

How should we discuss dietary requirements?

Tell La Distinction about guests’ allergies, intolerances and special diets when requesting a quote. The official menu describes adjustments and an allergy-management fee. Confirm specific needs, precautions and applicable charges with an advisor rather than assuming that a dish meets a dietary requirement.

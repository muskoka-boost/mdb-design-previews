A few practical details to bring to your planning conversation.

When is the seasonal menu in effect?

The published seasonal holiday menu is in effect from November 15 to January 15. The page identifies its holiday banquet selection as the 2026 menu. Confirm your desired date with an advisor.

What is the minimum group size?

The seasonal holiday menu specifies a minimum of 10 people. Share your approximate guest count when you enquire so an advisor can discuss your gathering.

How do the banquet and buffet differ?

The banquet is a plated, three-to-five-course meal served at the tables and paced for a seated event. The hot buffet is self-serve and more relaxed and flexible. Choose according to the style of your event and the number of guests.

What about allergies and special diets?

Tell La Distinction about allergies, intolerances and special diets when requesting a quote. The seasonal menu page describes adjustments and an allergy-management fee. Discuss the specific requirements and applicable charges with an advisor rather than assuming every dish is suitable.

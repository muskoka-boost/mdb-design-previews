Before you enquire

A few practical details.

Use these published menu details to prepare your questions. Your enquiry is the place to confirm the arrangements for your event.

When is the seasonal menu in effect?

The published seasonal holiday menu is in effect from November 15 to January 15. The page identifies its holiday banquet selection as the 2026 menu. Ask an advisor to confirm arrangements for your intended date.

What is the minimum group size?

The seasonal holiday menu specifies a minimum of 10 people. Include your approximate guest count when you enquire so an advisor can discuss your gathering.

Which format suits a seated corporate dinner?

The banquet is a plated, three-to-five-course meal served at the tables. The hot buffet is self-serve and described as more relaxed and flexible. La Distinction recommends choosing according to your event’s style and number of guests.

What should I share about dietary requirements?

Tell La Distinction about guests’ allergies, intolerances and special diets when requesting a quote. The seasonal menu page describes adjustments and an allergy-management fee. Discuss your guests’ specific needs and applicable charges with an advisor.

City streets, sea air, storybook places

This private experience connects a narrated Charlottetown drive with coastal views, Green Gables Heritage Place and PEI Preserve Company. An air-conditioned private van and a local guide keep transport and sightseeing together in one outing.

For families and friends planning an island visit, the useful question is simple: do these places fit the day you want to share?

The highlights below describe included experiences, not a confirmed stop-by-stop schedule.

Charlottetown

Start with the island’s historic streets.

Take a narrated drive through Charlottetown with a local guide. See St. Dunstan’s Basilica and Province House, and pass Government House, Victoria Park, UPEI and Queen Street.

It is the city chapter of an outing that also reaches beyond Charlottetown to the island’s coast and countryside.

Dalvay & Cavendish

Make room for the coast.

Enjoy scenic coastal views at Dalvay by the Sea and visit Cavendish Cliffs Outlook. PEI’s red sandstone coastline brings a different perspective after the city landmarks.

The tour’s coastal views sit alongside its history and literary stops, rather than being a separate excursion to arrange.

Green Gables Heritage Place

A familiar story,
a place to explore.

Explore the green-roofed farmhouse, period rooms and scenic trails at Green Gables Heritage Place, associated with Lucy Maud Montgomery’s Anne of Green Gables.

Attraction admissions are included in the private tour, so this literary landmark is part of the same island outing.

PEI Preserve Company

Gardens and local discoveries.

Walk through the gardens and browse local products at PEI Preserve Company. The riverside business offers specialty foods, blended full-leaf tea and spice blends.

This stop adds a different texture to a day of landmarks, coastal scenery and Green Gables.

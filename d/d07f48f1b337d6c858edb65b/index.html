Before you choose your dates.

The important details, without the small-print hunt. The resort’s current official terms govern any booking.

When must I book, and when can I stay?

The Winter Wins booking deadline is November 11, 2026. The eligible stay period is January 3–March 11, 2027. Those are different dates: the November deadline is for reserving your winter visit, not the start of the stay period.

Blackout dates may apply. Eligible accommodation is subject to availability and minimum-stay requirements.

Can I cancel or change my dates?

Full payment is due at booking. Winter Wins is non-refundable and non-cancellable, and date changes are not permitted. Consider whether your travel plans are firm before reserving.

What will be additional to the accommodation rate?

The published terms list 13% HST, 4% Municipal Accommodation Tax for Huntsville and a resort fee as additional. The indoor pool, fitness centre and listed included activities are benefits provided through that nightly resort fee.

Review the total for your selected accommodation and dates rather than treating the advertised accommodation savings as the final price.

Can I count on outdoor activities during my stay?

No individual outdoor activity should be treated as guaranteed. Weather, snow and ice conditions affect operation, and the listed activity seasons do not all cover the full Winter Wins travel period.

If a particular experience is central to your visit, ask the resort before committing to the non-refundable offer.

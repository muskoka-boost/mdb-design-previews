Winter Wins accommodation offer

More room for your winter plans.

Deerhurst Resort welcomes couples, families and groups to the shores of Peninsula Lake in Huntsville. Winter Wins is an advance-booking accommodation offer for eligible stays from January 3 to March 11, 2027.

The indoor pool and fitness centre are listed benefits of Winter Wins, included through the nightly resort fee. They offer another way to shape the day alongside your outdoor plans.

Know before
you make plans.

The booking deadline is in November. The getaway is in winter. Keep both in view, especially when considering the offer’s firm payment and cancellation conditions.

When must I book, and when can I stay?

The limited-time Winter Wins offer ends November 11, 2026. Eligible stay dates are January 3–March 11, 2027. Blackout dates may apply, and accommodation is subject to availability and minimum-stay requirements.

Can I cancel or change my dates?

Full payment is due at booking. Winter Wins is non-refundable and non-cancellable, with no date changes. Consider whether your plans are firm before making a reservation.

What charges are additional?

The published terms list 13% HST, 4% Municipal Accommodation Tax for Huntsville and a resort fee as additional. Review the total for your selected accommodation and dates rather than treating the accommodation discount as the final price.

Where is Deerhurst Resort?

Deerhurst Resort is on the shores of Peninsula Lake at 1235 Deerhurst Drive, Huntsville, Ontario, Canada P1H 2E8. Its official contact page provides contact details and a Maps & Directions link.

A Muskoka getaway with room for winter

On the shores of Peninsula Lake in Huntsville, Deerhurst Resort welcomes families, couples and groups. Shape your stay around time together: seasonal outdoor activities, the indoor pool, the fitness centre or on-site dining.

Winter Wins rewards advance planning with up to 30% off accommodation on eligible stays. Book by November 11, 2026, for stays January 3–March 11, 2027, using promo code WINTER1. Rates vary with your dates and accommodation, and some dates may require two or three nights.

Make sure your plans are firm

Full payment is due at booking. This offer is non-refundable and non-cancellable, with no date changes. Review the official conditions before making a reservation.

The accommodation discount is not the final total. Published terms list 13% HST, 4% Municipal Accommodation Tax for Huntsville and a resort fee as additional.

Keep an indoor option

The indoor pool and fitness centre are listed with Winter Wins. These benefits are included through the nightly resort fee, which is additional to the accommodation rate.

On-site dining offers another way to spend time at the resort. Do not assume meals are included in this accommodation offer.

Before you book

The details that shape your decision.

A little clarity now can help you decide whether Winter Wins fits your plans.

When must I book, and when can I stay?

The Winter Wins offer ends November 11, 2026. The stay period is January 3–March 11, 2027. These are different windows: November is for booking, not the start of the eligible travel period. Blackout dates may apply, and accommodation is subject to availability and minimum-stay requirements.

Can I cancel or change my dates?

Full payment is due at booking. Winter Wins is non-refundable and non-cancellable, with no date changes. Consider whether your travel plans are firm before making a reservation.

What should I include in the total cost?

The published terms list 13% HST, 4% Municipal Accommodation Tax for Huntsville and a resort fee as additional. Rates are per room, per night, based on double occupancy, with up to two children aged 18 and under sharing their parents’ room free. Check the total for your selected dates, room and party size rather than treating the accommodation saving as the final price.

Where is Deerhurst, and who can answer my questions?

Deerhurst Resort is at 1235 Deerhurst Drive, Huntsville, Ontario, Canada P1H 2E8, on the shores of Peninsula Lake. The resort’s official contact page provides contact details and a Maps & Directions link. Ask the resort about your intended stay before booking if any offer condition is unclear.

The official package page already presents Winter Wins, its promo code, terms and a Book Now route. This concept does not propose replacing that booking solution. The possible benefit is a clearer offer explanation for couples and families assessing their dates. Current advertising, traffic, demand, buying intent and conversion performance are unknown; this example does not establish a promotional gap or predict results.

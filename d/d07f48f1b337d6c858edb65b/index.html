Your stay, considered

A winter escape.
Not a winter rush.

Deerhurst Resort sits on the shores of Peninsula Lake in Huntsville, Ontario, welcoming families, couples and groups. Shape your getaway around time together: seasonal outdoor experiences, a visit to the indoor pool or a simpler day with on-site dining.

Start with your accommodation

Winter Wins offers up to 30% off accommodation when booked in advance using WINTER1. The November deadline is for booking; the getaway itself falls between January 3 and March 11, 2027.

Eligible rooms and rates depend on your dates and availability. Blackout dates and minimum stays apply; selected dates may require two or three nights. Review the official offer before choosing your stay.

Firm plans.
Clear conditions.

The accommodation saving is only one part of the decision. Check the commitment and full cost before booking.

Payment & changes

Full payment is due at the time of booking. This offer is non-refundable and non-cancellable, with no date changes.

Additional charges

13% HST, 4% Municipal Accommodation Tax for Huntsville and a resort fee are additional. Review the total for your dates and accommodation.

Room & stay terms

Published rates are per room, per night, based on double occupancy: two adults per room, with up to two children aged 18 and under sharing their parents’ room free. Select accommodations, availability, blackout dates and minimum-stay requirements apply.

Before you choose
your dates.

Is November 11 the last day I can stay?

No. November 11, 2026 is the booking deadline. The eligible stay period is January 3–March 11, 2027, subject to availability, blackout dates and the offer’s other conditions.

What if my plans change?

Winter Wins requires full payment at booking and does not allow cancellation, refunds or date changes. Consider whether your travel plans are firm before making a reservation.

Are activities and taxes part of the room rate?

The listed activities, indoor pool and fitness centre are included through the nightly resort fee. That fee is additional to the accommodation rate, as are 13% HST and 4% Municipal Accommodation Tax. Outdoor activities remain dependent on weather, snow and ice.

Where is the resort, and how can I ask a question?

Deerhurst Resort is at 1235 Deerhurst Drive, Huntsville, Ontario, Canada P1H 2E8, on Peninsula Lake. The official contact URL redirects to the resort’s homeowners contact directory. General resort contact details and a Maps & Directions link appear in that page’s footer; it is not a dedicated Winter Wins enquiry page.

Why this offer

The official Winter Wins page identifies a concrete package, deadline, travel period, promo code and existing booking route. This example proposes a deadline-led message alongside that route—not a replacement booking system or a claim that current promotion is inadequate.

Traffic, advertising performance, audience demand and buying intent have not been measured here. Is promoting Winter Wins before the November 11 deadline a priority?

Around your stay

Fresh air.
A change of pace.

Deerhurst Resort welcomes families, couples and groups to the shores of Peninsula Lake in Huntsville. Shape your winter getaway around time together, with seasonal outdoor experiences, indoor recreation and on-site dining.

Follow winter outdoors

Deerhurst lists snowshoeing and Maple Lawn ice skating for December–March. Cross-country skiing is listed for mid-January–early March, so the timing of your stay matters.

These seasonal experiences are included through the nightly resort fee. Outdoor activities depend on weather, snow and ice conditions; not every activity will necessarily operate during your visit.

Keep an indoor option

The Winter Wins offer lists an indoor pool and fitness centre. They offer another way to spend part of your day, whether you prefer indoor plans or a break between outdoor activities.

These benefits are included through the nightly resort fee, which is additional to the accommodation rate.

A few things to know before you book.

When do I book, and when do I travel?

Book by November 11, 2026. The Winter Wins stay period is January 3–March 11, 2027. These are separate dates: the booking deadline comes first. Eligible accommodations are subject to availability, and blackout dates may apply.

Can I cancel or move my dates?

No. The published Winter Wins terms require full payment at booking and state that the offer is non-refundable and non-cancellable, with no date changes. Consider whether your travel plans are firm before reserving.

What should I include in my budget?

Include the accommodation rate plus 13% HST, 4% Municipal Accommodation Tax for Huntsville and the resort fee. Rates may fluctuate with accommodation, dates and availability. Review the total for your chosen stay rather than treating the discount as a final price.

Where is the resort, and how do I ask a question?

Deerhurst Resort is at 1235 Deerhurst Drive, Huntsville, Ontario, Canada P1H 2E8, on Peninsula Lake.

Official offer code WINTER1

The booking deadline is in November. The getaway is in January, February or March.

Winter Wins rewards advance planning for a Deerhurst stay. The offer applies to select accommodations, with rates varying by date and availability. Blackout dates and minimum-stay requirements apply; some dates may require two or three nights.

Deerhurst welcomes families, couples and groups to Peninsula Lake in Huntsville. Time outdoors, an indoor pool, a fitness centre and on-site dining give you different ways to shape your stay, whether you want seasonal activities or a simpler change of scene.

Before you make plans

Know the dates.
Know the details.

A little clarity now can help you decide whether this advance-booking offer suits your trip.

When must I book, and when can I stay?

The Winter Wins offer ends November 11, 2026, for stays January 3–March 11, 2027. Those are two different planning windows: reserve before the November deadline for an eligible winter stay. Blackout dates may apply, and accommodation is subject to availability.

Can I cancel or change my dates?

Full payment is due at booking. Winter Wins is non-refundable and non-cancellable, with no date changes. Consider whether your plans are firm before making a reservation.

What should I include in the total cost?

The published terms list 13% HST, 4% Municipal Accommodation Tax for Huntsville and a resort fee as additional. Rates apply to select accommodations and can fluctuate by date and availability. Check the total for your selected stay rather than treating the accommodation discount as the final price.

Where is Deerhurst Resort?

Deerhurst is on the shores of Peninsula Lake at 1235 Deerhurst Drive, Huntsville, Ontario, Canada P1H 2E8. Its official contact page provides contact details and a Maps & Directions link.
